# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-XX

- Official release!
- (NEW!) Boon icons for each Hermes Duo Boons
- Balancing
    - Changed various requirements from Hermes' side
    - High Tension (Zeus x Hermes): Increased chain-lightning damage (10 -> 30)
    - Hurricane Eye (Demeter x Hermes): Decreased Gusts damage (10 -> 7)
    - Hurricane Eye (Demeter x Hermes): Limited number of pairs of Gusts that can be present at once (unlimited -> up to 3)
    - Hurricane Eye (Demeter x Hermes): Increased Gusts travel rotation (450 -> 750)
    - Golden Prodigy (Apollo x Hermes): Life restoration now only occurs with damage dealt by your Weapon while standing in your Casts
    - Aerobic Capacity (Hestia x Hermes): Decreased fireball damage (250 -> 80)
- Bug fixes:
    - High Tension (Zeus x Hermes): Fixed an issue where the chain-lightning would be unaffected by Electric Overload (Zeus)
    - High Tension (Zeus x Hermes): Fixed an issue where the chain-lightning would be unaffected by Master Conductor (Zeus x Hephaestus)
